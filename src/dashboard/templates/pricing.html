FAQ
Common questions
Can I switch plans at any time? ▾
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
Do I need to provide API keys for each platform? ▾
Yes. Each platform (X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) requires your own credentials in Settings. We never share accounts between users. Telegram only needs a bot token.
Is avatar video included in Pro? ▾
Yes — D-ID avatar videos are included in Pro. You need your own D-ID API key (Lite plan ~$9/month) and optionally ElevenLabs for voice.
What niches are supported? ▾
10 niches: Crypto, Fitness, Business, Food, Fashion, Travel, Technology, Marketing, Real Estate, Entertainment. Each niche has its own news sources, hashtags, image styles, and tone.
